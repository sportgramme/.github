# Glossary — D–F

**Index:** [A–C](A-C.md) · D–F · [G–I](G-I.md) · [J–L](J-L.md) · [M–O](M-O.md) · [P–R](P-R.md) · [S–U](S-U.md) · [V–Z](V-Z.md) · [about](README.md)

### Decision rule

The condition, fixed *before* a **[hypothesis](G-I.md#hypothesis)** is run, that
turns its test result into a **[verdict](V-Z.md#verdict)**. It must combine
statistical significance *and* real-world magnitude — never a bare p-value, and
never a judgement made after seeing the result.

### Delivery mechanism

One way content reaches a **[syndication partner](S-U.md#syndication-partner)**: a
**[channel](A-C.md#channel-syndication)** with a type, a purpose, a target
environment (production / test / development) and a priority. A partner can have
several, so different content streams go by different routes and test traffic
never goes out on a production channel.

### Division (organisational)

A business or organisational boundary — including sport-family boundaries — that
access can be scoped to. A grant in the **[access matrix](A-C.md#access-matrix)**
can be as wide as the whole platform or as narrow as one division.

### Drifter

A runner whose price is getting longer (bigger odds) over time — the market
pushing it out. Opposite of a **[steamer](S-U.md#steamer)**.

### Effect size

A measure of *how big* a difference is, in interpretable units and independent of
sample size (for example ε², Cliff's δ, a variance ratio). Every test in the
framework reports one alongside its p-value.

### Exchange (betting)

A marketplace where customers bet against each other rather than against a
bookmaker — Betfair, Matchbook. Its prices move differently from fixed-odds
firms, so analyses treat exchanges as a separate group.

### Feed assembly

The step that stages a published feature and its lead image into a
**[proofing area](P-R.md#proofing-area)**, then builds it into the relevant
sport's feed and the **[consolidated feed](A-C.md#consolidated-feed)**, then
pushes to the public site — removing the contributor's local draft only once
delivery is confirmed.

### Focal point

Interest data attached to an image so hero and card crops keep the subject in
frame at whatever aspect ratio a layout needs — faces and action are not cropped
out on phones or wide screens.
