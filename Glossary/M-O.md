### Manifest (media batch)

The platform's own record of the files in an upload batch, its identity, its
**[trust tier](S-U.md#trust-level--trusted--untrusted)** and its destination —
all assigned by the platform, not the contributor's device. A batch's final state
is settled against the manifest, so a lost network response never leaves a
delivered batch looking failed.

### Market / runner

A market is a race; a runner is a horse in it. Betting analyses are usually
grained at market × runner × bookmaker.

### Moderation gate

The automated check probationary contributions pass through before they can be
promoted from **[quarantine](P-R.md#quarantine)** into the
**[governed asset library](G-I.md#governed-asset-library)**. Trusted
contributions skip it.

### Overround

The amount a bookmaker's **[book percentage](A-C.md#book-percentage)** exceeds
1.0 — its built-in margin across a race. A book percentage *below* 1.0 is an
**[arbitrage](A-C.md#arbitrage)**.
